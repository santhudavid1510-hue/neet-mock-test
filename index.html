Q1. (Cell – Unit of Life) Which of the following cell organelles has its own DNA and ribosomes?
Biology · Cell
Correct: Mitochondria are semi-autonomous with circular DNA and 70S ribosomes.
Chloroplasts also have their own DNA, but among the given options mitochondria is correct.
Q2. (Cell – Unit of Life) The fluid mosaic model of plasma membrane was proposed by:
Biology · Cell
Correct: Singer and Nicolson proposed the fluid mosaic model.
It describes proteins floating in a fluid lipid bilayer like icebergs in a sea.
Q3. (Cell Cycle & Division) Crossing over during meiosis occurs in:
Biology · Cell Cycle
Correct: Crossing over occurs during pachytene of prophase I.
At pachytene, non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes exchange segments at chiasmata.
Q4. (Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants) In angiosperms, the product of triple fusion is:
Biology · SRFP
Correct: Triple fusion gives triploid primary endosperm nucleus which forms endosperm.
Double fertilisation = syngamy (zygote) + triple fusion (endosperm).
Q5. (Human Reproduction) In humans, implantation of the blastocyst normally occurs in:
Biology · Human Reproduction
Correct: Blastocyst implants in the thickened endometrium.
Progesterone prepares the endometrium for implantation and placenta formation.
Q6. (Animal Kingdom) Which of the following is diploblastic and used as a model for regeneration studies?
Biology · Animal Kingdom
Correct: Hydra (phylum Cnidaria) is diploblastic and highly regenerative.
Cnidarians have ectoderm and endoderm with mesoglea in between.
Q7. (Cell Cycle) During the cell cycle, DNA replication occurs in:
Biology · Cell Cycle
Correct: DNA replication occurs during S (synthetic) phase.
In S phase, DNA content doubles from 2C to 4C, but chromosome number remains same.
Q8. (Human Reproduction) In human females, the surge of which hormone induces ovulation?
Biology · Human Reproduction
Correct: The mid-cycle LH surge triggers ovulation.
FSH mainly stimulates follicular growth, while LH surge causes rupture of Graafian follicle.
Q9. (Reproductive Health) Which contraceptive method also offers protection against sexually transmitted infections (STIs)?
Biology · Reproductive Health
Correct: Condoms act as a barrier and reduce chances of STIs.
Most other methods only prevent conception, not transmission of infections.
Q10. (SRFP) In an ovule, the opening through which pollen tube usually enters is called:
Biology · SRFP
Correct: Pollen tube commonly enters the ovule through the micropyle.
This type of fertilisation is called porogamy.
Q11. (Chemical Bonding) Which of the following molecules is linear due to sp hybridisation of the central atom?
Chemistry · Bonding
Correct: Be in gaseous BeCl₂ is sp hybridised, giving linear geometry.
NH₃ and H₂O are sp³, BF₃ is sp² (trigonal planar).
Q12. (Bonding / GOC) The total number of σ (sigma) and π (pi) bonds in ethyne (acetylene), HC≡CH, is:
Chemistry · Bonding
Correct: Ethyne has 3 σ-bonds and 2 π-bonds.
Each C–H is one σ (2 total) and C≡C has 1σ + 2π.
Q13. (IUPAC Nomenclature) The IUPAC name of (CH3)2CH–CH2–CH3 is:
Chemistry · IUPAC
Correct: Longest chain has 5 carbons without any substituent → pentane.
Recount the main chain from either side; you can include all five carbons straight.
Q14. (Isomerism) Which molecular formula can show both chain and position isomerism?
Chemistry · Isomerism
Correct: C₄H₁₀O (alcohols/ethers) shows both chain and position isomerism.
Butan-1-ol, butan-2-ol (position) and 2-methylpropan-1-ol (chain) are examples.
Q15. (GOC) Which statement about +I effect (positive inductive effect) is correct?
Chemistry · GOC
Correct: Alkyl groups are electron-releasing via +I effect.
–NO₂ is strongly –I (electron-withdrawing); inductive effect works in σ-bonded systems in general.
Q16. (GOC / Acidity) Which of the following is the most acidic?
Chemistry · GOC
Correct: Terminal alkyne (ethyne) is more acidic due to sp-hybridised carbon.
Greater s-character stabilises the conjugate base through higher electronegativity.
Q17. (Chemical Bonding) Which species has a lone pair on the central atom and a trigonal pyramidal shape?
Chemistry · Bonding
Correct: NH₃ has three bond pairs and one lone pair (sp³) giving trigonal pyramidal shape.
BF₃ is trigonal planar, CH₄ is tetrahedral, CO₂ is linear.
Q18. (IUPAC) The IUPAC name of CH3–CH2–CH2–CH3 is:
Chemistry · IUPAC
Correct: Straight chain of four carbons with no substituent → butane.
Propane has three carbons, pentane has five.
Q19. (Isomerism) Which of the following can show geometrical (cis–trans) isomerism?
Chemistry · Isomerism
Correct: But-2-ene (CH₃–CH=CH–CH₃) can exist as cis and trans isomers.
For geometrical isomerism, each carbon of the double bond must have two different substituents.
Q20. (GOC / Resonance) Which statement about resonance is correct?
Chemistry · GOC
Correct: Resonance is delocalisation of π (and sometimes lone-pair) electrons over two or more atoms.
Resonance forms are imaginary; only the delocalised hybrid has real existence and is more stable.
Q21. (Dual Nature) The de Broglie wavelength of a particle of momentum p is:
Physics · Dual Nature
Correct: de Broglie relation is λ = h / p.
Here h is Planck’s constant and p is the linear momentum of the particle.
Q22. (Photoelectric Effect) In a photoelectric experiment, when light of higher intensity but same frequency is used, which of the following increases?
Physics · Dual Nature
Correct: Higher intensity → more photons → more photoelectrons per second.
Stopping potential and maximum KE depend on frequency, not intensity.
Q23. (Atoms) In Bohr’s model of hydrogen atom, the radius of the nth orbit is proportional to:
Physics · Atoms
Correct: rₙ ∝ n² for hydrogen-like species.
rₙ = a₀ n² / Z, where a₀ is Bohr radius and Z is atomic number.
Q24. (Atoms / Spectra) A spectral line in hydrogen arises due to transition of an electron from n = 4 to n = 2. This line belongs to:
Physics · Atoms
Correct: Balmer series corresponds to transitions ending at n = 2.
Lyman (n=1), Balmer (n=2), Paschen (n=3), Brackett (n=4), Pfund (n=5) etc.
Q25. (Basic Math / Units) If displacement x is measured in metres and time t in seconds, then the slope of x–t graph has the unit:
Physics · Basic Math
Correct: Slope = Δx/Δt has unit m/s (velocity).
Dimensional analysis: [x]/[t] = L/T.
Q26. (Dual Nature / Threshold Frequency) The relation between work function φ and threshold frequency ν0 is:
Physics · Dual Nature
Correct: Work function φ = hν₀.
At threshold frequency, KEmax = 0, so hν₀ = φ.
Q27. (Dual Nature) If the kinetic energy of an electron increases, its de Broglie wavelength:
Physics · Dual Nature
Correct: λ = h/√(2mK), so increasing kinetic energy reduces wavelength.
Momentum p ∝ √K, so λ ∝ 1/√K.
Q28. (Basic Math / Errors) If Q = a b² / c and percentage errors in a, b and c are 1%, 2% and 3% respectively, the percentage error in Q is:
Physics · Basic Math
Correct: ΔQ/Q = Δa/a + 2Δb/b + Δc/c = 1 + 4 + 3 = 8%.
For powers, percentage error is multiplied by the power.
Q29. (Atoms) According to Bohr’s model, the energy of an electron in a stable orbit of hydrogen atom is:
Physics · Atoms
Correct: Energy levels of bound electrons are negative (Eₙ < 0).
Eₙ = −13.6 Z² / n² eV for hydrogen-like atoms.
Q30. (Atoms / Spectral Lines) When an electron de-excites from n = 4 to n = 1, the total number of different spectral lines emitted is:
Physics · Atoms
Correct: Number of lines = n(n − 1)/2 = 4 × 3 / 2 = 6.
Possible transitions: 4→3, 4→2, 4→1, 3→2, 3→1, 2→1.
